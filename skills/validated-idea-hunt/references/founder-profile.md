# Founder profile

Edit this file to change what the hunt looks for and how it scores. Every skill in the set reads it.

## Problem spaces, in priority order

1. Designers and Figma users. Sub-spaces to hunt in:
   - solo designers at early-stage startups
   - designers handing off to developers who build with AI coding tools
   - designers doing their own user research
2. Contract and invoicing gaps for small businesses, niche within niche.

Idea already on the table (use Check mode): Figma-to-email export for marketers, starting with Klaviyo email agencies.

## What a good idea looks like

- A simple, frequent, tedious step in an existing workflow that is hard to automate today.
- Strictly vertical: a very specific niche, ideally a niche within a niche.
- One or two core flows. AI may automate the tedious part, but the product stays simple.
- Ranked by pain intensity first.

## Constraints

- Solo founder with 20+ hours a week for product work alongside contract work.
- Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Clerk, Stripe, Vercel.
- Prefers the simplest, lowest-effort solution that works, and free or low-cost tools.
- Has never built a Figma plugin yet. Treat plugin APIs as a feasibility question, not a blocker.

## Distribution

- No distribution track record yet: no stranger has signed up or paid for something she made.
- Marketplace listings (Figma Community and similar app stores) are the channel she would sustain. Ideas that can live in a marketplace score higher on Distribution.

## Founder fit

- Understands designers best as an audience. Heavy Figma user.
- Strong at research: runs interviews and usability tests, has recruited strangers herself.
- Will embed in a niche online; in person only with people she already knows.
- Only surface-level knowledge of client industries. Her own freelance admin doesn't hurt much, so don't assume she feels freelancer-admin pain firsthand.

## Validation style

- 5–8 short interviews with the target niche plus one clickable prototype or small coded demo, before any full build.
- Stop researching once the remaining uncertainty is about behaviour, which only real users can answer.
