# Scoring and Cheapest Tests

Purpose: scoring rubric and test design for `find-wedge` steps 7-8.

## Scoring (1-5 each)

Suggested dimensions (recommendation; adjust with the founder, weights optional): buyer reachability, pain frequency/cost, willingness-to-pay signal, incumbent constraint, switching reason, expansion path, founder fit, time to first paid test.

Report the weighted score plus the one-line judgement that may override it.

## Test ladder (cheapest first)

1. Search/community: do buyers discuss the problem with commercial-intent language?
2. Landing page: wedge promise vs generic category promise.
3. Outbound/community: post, DM, or email a small targeted group with a specific offer.
4. Paid pilot: fixed scope, fixed price, or deposit.
5. Concierge: deliver the outcome manually to 3-5 customers before building.

Each test states: audience, channel, offer, success metric, sample size, kill criteria.
