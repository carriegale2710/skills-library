# Cold Email Writer

## When to Use This Skill

Use when the user wants to email a named hiring manager, founder or recruiter with no referral, or mentions: "cold email", "reach out", "intro email", "outreach". For a letter sent as an application, use `cover-letter` (speculative). For a referral, use its referral type.

The email should read like a person who noticed something specific about the company, not a candidate broadcasting credentials.

## Before writing

1. Get one specific thing from the company's site, ad or `company-research` output. Never invent company details.
2. Get the recipient's name. Ask if the user doesn't have it.
3. Pick the one or two most relevant things from the master resume. Never invent facts or metrics.

## Structure (150-300 words)

- **Subject:** `Quick intro — [First Last]`
- **Hook (2-3 sentences):** the specific thing that pulled the user in, such as a number, a product decision or a technical choice. Not "I love what you're building".
- **Location (only if in a different country):** say it early and plainly.
- **Experience gap (only if the ad asks for more years):** one sentence. Say what the user has done and invite them to judge it. Do not apologise.
- **Who they are (3-5 sentences):** current role, relevant past, one or two projects. Context, not an achievement list.
- **Connection (1-2 sentences):** what the role needs and how the user's work matches. State it as an observation.
- **Link:** one line with the portfolio URL, if there is one.
- **Close:** low-pressure and confident. Name and email.

## Avoid

- "I am writing to express my interest in..."
- "Please find attached my resume"
- "I would love the opportunity to..."
- "I am excited to potentially join..."
- "I would be an exceptional addition", "I know I may not have all the experience..."
- Anything that could apply to any company

## Output

Put the final email in a plain code block for copy-paste, starting with the `Subject:` line. Add the recipient and company to `tracker.md`, with a follow-up date.
