# Selection criteria glossary

Pitch the evidence to the level the wording asks for. From strongest to weakest:

| Wording | What you must show |
|---|---|
| Proven record in | Claims you can back up, preferably with documented outcomes |
| Experience in | You have performed the task |
| Ability to | You have the skills or knowledge, and have done such tasks before |
| Capacity to | You are able or qualified to do it; transferable skills count |
| Understanding of | More than knowledge: you grasp the matter and why it matters |
| Knowledge of | Familiarity from actual experience or from learning |
| Background in | Usually your qualification or area of specialisation |
| Aptitude for | Suitability for the task; potential, not yet proven |
| Awareness of | The least familiarity: you know it exists |

**Qualifiers** such as "well developed", "demonstrated", "extensive" and "high level" mean every claim needs concrete examples that show breadth and depth.
