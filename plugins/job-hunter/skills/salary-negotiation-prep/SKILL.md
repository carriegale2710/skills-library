# Salary Negotiation Prep

## When to Use This Skill

Use after the user has an offer, or asks how much to ask for, or mentions: "salary negotiation", "negotiate offer", "counter offer", "compensation".

Do not put salary in any application document unless the ad asks for it. If it does, say it is negotiable and you are happy to discuss it at interview.

Never invent market figures. Use only numbers the user found or that you cite with a source. If you can't search, say so and ask for the user's research.

## 1. Market range

Check several sources, such as the employer's own salary band or award rate if it is published, Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary, PayScale, Levels.fyi (tech) and recruiter salary guides. Build a range for the role, location and experience: low, target (median), high, stretch.

## 2. Total compensation

Base + bonus + equity (and its vesting) + retirement contributions + benefits. Compare the whole package, not just base.

Also negotiable: signing bonus, equity, title, leave, remote work, development budget, start date and an earlier review.

## 3. Leverage

Raises the ask: relevant experience, rare skills, results the user can evidence, a competing offer, a referral. Lowers it: entry level, a career change, skill gaps. Be honest with the user about which applies.

## 4. Process

1. Verbal offer: express enthusiasm, ask for it in writing and for the deadline.
2. Written offer: thank them and take 24-48 hours.
3. Counter with a specific number and a reason.
4. Expect more than one round. Get the final terms in writing.

## Scripts

Email:

```
Subject: [Name] - Offer discussion

Hi [Name],

Thank you for the offer to join [Company] as [Title]. I'm excited about [specific thing about the role].

Having reviewed the offer, I'd like to discuss base salary. Based on my research for this role and my [specific experience or skill], I was hoping for $[ask] rather than $[offer]. [One line of justification.]

I'm open to discussing the rest of the package too. Do you have time for a quick call?

Thank you again,
[Name]
```

Call (counter): "I'm excited about this role. Based on my research and [evidence], I was hoping for a base closer to $[amount]. Is there flexibility there?" Then stop and listen.

If they push back on base: "Could we look at a signing bonus, equity or [other] to bridge the gap?"

If asked for salary expectations first: "I'm focused on fit. What range have you budgeted for this role?" If pressed, give the researched range.

If asked for current salary: where the law restricts the question, decline. Otherwise redirect to the market range for the role.

Competing offer: mention one only if it is real, as information, not a threat.

## Don't

Accept on the spot, lie about offers, make ultimatums, accept verbal promises without writing, or negotiate without a researched number.

## Output

- Market range with sources
- The offer as a table (component, amount) and total Year 1
- The counter: component, ask and justification
- Email or call script for this situation
- Plan B elements and the user's walk-away point (the user decides it)
- Questions to clarify: vesting, whether the bonus is guaranteed, review cycle
