# Career profile

Write this section last. It summarises the strongest content already in the tailored resume.

## Format

3-5 bullet points; 3-4 when space is tight. Each point states **what** (skill, experience or outcome) and **where** (organisation, project or context). Together they highlight the top selling points and address the vital criteria, showing depth and breadth.

Example shape (illustrative only):
- Front-end developer with 2 years of contract work building React and Next.js interfaces for 5 clients
- Shipped a solo SaaS product end to end: Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Stripe, deployed on Vercel
- Combines UX/UI design and engineering: designs in Figma, then builds and tests the production UI
- Highly developed organisational skills from leading a 4-person hackathon team at <event>

## Rules

1. Write in summary statements, not achievement statements. No first person ("I").
2. Point 1 answers the role directly: who you are relative to this job.
3. Cover the vital criteria from `criteria.md`, using its keywords, and the strongest selling points from `resume/master-resume.md`.
4. Every claim must already appear, with evidence, elsewhere in the resume.
5. One line per point.
6. No objective statements ("Seeking a challenging position…"). No self-descriptions without evidence ("hard-working", "results-oriented", "team player", "motivated").
