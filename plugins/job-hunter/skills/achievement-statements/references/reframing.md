# Reframing without inventing

Reframing changes how a true fact is worded, not the fact. Every reframed bullet must still match `resume/master-resume.md`. If the reframe needs a fact that is not recorded there, ask the user first.

Adapted from `matching-strategies.md` in [resume-tailoring-skill](https://github.com/varunr89/resume-tailoring-skill), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Varun Ramesh.

## Four strategies

**1. Match their words.** Use the ad's or the organisation's term for the same thing (see the keywords and organisation language in `criteria.md`).

- Before: "Ran user interviews and usability tests"
- After: "Led user research, including interviews and usability testing"
- Why: the ad says "user research"

**2. Shift the emphasis.** Lead with the part of the fact the role values most.

- Before: "Wrote SQL queries to find duplicate customer records, saving 5 hours a week"
- After: "Saved the support team 5 hours a week by finding duplicate customer records with SQL"
- Why: the role values business outcomes over methods

**3. Adjust the technical detail.** Name the tools when the ad names them. Drop them when the ad is tool-agnostic.

- Tool-agnostic role: "Built an automated test suite for the checkout flow"
- Tool-specific role: "Built an automated test suite for the checkout flow (Playwright, GitHub Actions)"

**4. Bring out the relevant scale.** Pick the dimension of scale the role cares about.

- Before: "Ran a project with 3 stakeholders"
- After: "Coordinated a project across 3 teams: design, engineering and marketing"
- Why: the role wants cross-team work, and the 3 stakeholders were from 3 teams

## Not allowed

- Upgrading your part: "helped" becomes "led" only if you led it.
- Inflating numbers, or rounding up past what happened.
- Claiming a tool, method or domain you only watched others use.
- Changing a job title to one the employer never gave you. You may add a descriptor, for example "Intern (Front-end)", if it describes the actual work.
