# Action verbs by skill signal

Pick the verb that signals the skill the job ad asks for.

## Building / engineering
Architected, Automated, Built, Debugged, Deployed, Developed, Engineered, Implemented, Integrated, Migrated, Optimised, Prototyped, Refactored, Shipped, Tested

## Design / creative
Conceptualised, Created, Customised, Designed, Illustrated, Mocked up, Modelled, Redesigned, Revised, Shaped, Sketched, Wireframed

## Analysis / research
Analysed, Audited, Benchmarked, Compared, Diagnosed, Evaluated, Investigated, Measured, Researched, Surveyed, Synthesised, Validated

## Leadership
Coordinated, Delegated, Directed, Established, Initiated, Launched, Led, Managed, Mentored, Motivated, Oversaw, Spearheaded, Supervised

## Communication / collaboration
Collaborated, Consulted, Documented, Facilitated, Negotiated, Persuaded, Presented, Proposed, Wrote

## Organising / delivery
Delivered, Executed, Planned, Prioritised, Scheduled, Standardised, Streamlined, Systematised, Tracked

## Improvement
Accelerated, Cut, Enhanced, Grew, Improved, Increased, Reduced, Saved, Simplified, Strengthened

## Problem solving
Eliminated, Fixed, Mitigated, Prevented, Resolved, Troubleshot

## Achievement
Achieved, Awarded, Earned, Exceeded, Secured, Won

## Teaching / helping
Advised, Coached, Guided, Onboarded, Supported, Trained, Tutored

## Avoid as openers
Assisted (unless accurate), Handled, Helped, Responsible for, Utilised, Was involved in, Worked on
